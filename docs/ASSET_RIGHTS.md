# Petal: assets and rights

Last checked: October 2026. This is a list for your own records, not legal advice.

## Images, video, voice, music
None. The app has no photos, videos, voice recordings, or music, and no AI-generated people or voices. Because of that, no likeness consent or "AI-generated" disclosure is needed.
