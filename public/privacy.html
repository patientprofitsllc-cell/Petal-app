Petal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version: Petal does not collect your personal information. What you save stays on your device.

Who we are

Petal is published by [YOUR COMPANY NAME]. You can reach us at [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL].

What Petal collects

Nothing. Petal has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, age, location, photos, contacts, health details, or the dates of your period. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, or notifications.

What Petal saves on your device

Petal saves two small lists in the app's local storage on your device: the lessons you have finished, and the lessons you have bookmarked. Your care checklist is only kept until you close the app. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Network use

The iPhone app works without an internet connection and does not send any data out. If you use the Petal website instead, the company that hosts the website may keep standard technical request logs, such as your IP address and the time of your visit. We do not use those logs to identify you.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information, because we do not have it. The links to ACOG, CDC, and FDA pages open in your browser, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

Your saved lessons and progress stay on your device until you remove them. To remove them, open Privacy & sources in the app and tap "Clear saved lessons & progress", or delete the app.

Children and teens

Petal is written for teens and young adults. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing about a child for us to store or share. If you are young, it is a good idea to read this page with a trusted adult.

Health information

Petal gives general education, not medical advice. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or predict anything. For heavy bleeding, severe pain, or anything that worries you, talk to a doctor or nurse.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
